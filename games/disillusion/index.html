How to play

Take the tile on the left and place it into a gap on the board.

  • Tall tile → it must match the color of every tile it touches.
  • Wide tile → it must match the symbol of every tile it touches.
  • It also has to physically fit the gap — shaded gaps are the legal drops.

A wrong drop locks that gap for a few seconds. Match enough tiles and clear 4 boards before the timer runs out to unlock the next level.
